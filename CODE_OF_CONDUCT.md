# Code of Conduct

Metaplate's community should be a welcoming, safe place to collaborate. Treat
other participants with respect, assume good intent, and critique ideas and
code without attacking the person behind them.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and constructive.
- Respect different backgrounds, identities, experiences, and viewpoints.
- Give actionable technical feedback and accept it professionally.
- Keep public discussions relevant to the project.
- Protect private information and follow responsible disclosure practices.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption, and other abusive behavior are not
acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. They
will consider context, impact, and patterns of behavior when responding.

Report conduct concerns privately to
[lnorton89@gmail.com](mailto:lnorton89@gmail.com). Include links or screenshots
that establish the relevant context. Reports will be handled as confidentially
as practical; retaliation against someone who makes a good-faith report is
itself a violation.

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
and other spaces where someone is representing the Metaplate project.
